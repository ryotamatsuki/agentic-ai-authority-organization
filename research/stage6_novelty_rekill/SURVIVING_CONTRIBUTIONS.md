# Surviving Contributions

Stage: 6R Proposition-Level Novelty Re-Kill
Date: 2026-09-03

## Canonical status

**No current Proposition survives as an independent main theoretical contribution.**

This file deliberately distinguishes facts that remain true/useful from claims that survive the novelty gate.

## What remains valid as model content

1. The paper offers a compact two-period formulation combining external AI delegation, a recovery-relevant internal experience stock, external-platform unavailability, and a private-price/resource-cost distinction.
2. The `recovery capability` interpretation is coherent: retained human authority preserves experience that becomes valuable in abnormal states.
3. P1's concavity/cutoff characterization is mathematically useful for solving the model.
4. P2 correctly identifies a parameter region in which current task-level AI advantage coexists with retained human authority.
5. P3 correctly identifies the direct accounting wedge `n(p-r)` and the direction of the private-versus-resource-cost cutoff under interiority.
6. P4 correctly provides a monotone capability comparative static and, after correcting the `kappa=1` domain issue, a sufficient uniform-persistence bound.
7. No single located prior paper exactly reproduces every primitive in the complete current setup.

## What does NOT survive as novelty

The statements above are not enough for Stage 6 `GO`. They are either:

- model characterization,
- institution-specific packaging of known economic mechanisms,
- mechanical consequences of the benchmark definition, or
- sharpenings that do not create a new strategic/dynamic feedback.

## Residual application value

The application can still be useful. The paper organizes a real emerging organizational problem: firms may delegate execution to externally supplied AI while needing to retain enough human experience to recover when the external service fails or becomes unreliable.

Under the canonical workflow this is currently classified as **application/institutional value**, not a surviving theory contribution.

A future version can regain a theory contribution only if an earlier-stage pivot creates and verifies one new interaction theorem that cannot be obtained from dynamic outsourcing, disruption/concurrent sourcing, or AI-learning benchmarks separately.

## Provisional contribution statement if the current model were described without claiming novelty

> The model provides a parsimonious application of dynamic sourcing and capability-retention logic to externally supplied agentic AI, making explicit how current AI delegation can trade off against internal recovery capacity and how a private access price differs from a resource-cost benchmark.

This statement is accurate but is **not sufficient for Stage 6 GO** and should not be used to bypass the NO-GO verdict.
