## Whole-game conclusion

No single paper above is `EXACT PRIOR ART` for the complete current game. The current model requires stitching together distinct sourcing and AI-learning literatures. That fact prevents an exact-absorption verdict.

However, the current paper still fails the Stage 6 interaction test: the stitched architecture does not create a new strategic feedback or a full-model-only theorem. The outage term multiplies the value of the same retained capability margin; the price wedge is additive; and `n` is a scaling/amplification parameter. The headline outcomes already occur in the close benchmarks.
