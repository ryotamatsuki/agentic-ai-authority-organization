## 5. Proposition-targeted findings

### P1 search outcome

Searches for partial outsourcing, make-and-buy, hybrid AI governance, and partial AI delegation found direct antecedents. Hybrid organization is not independently novel.

### P2 search outcome

The strongest direct analogue is Gray et al. (2009): internal production may be retained despite a current cost disadvantage because outsourcing changes learning and future costs. Anderson & Parker (2002) independently establish long-run learning losses from outsourcing and partial-outsource dominance. Cassidey et al. (2022) supplies the disruption/backup-capacity analogue.

### P3 search outcome

Supplier markup and internal sourcing are already central to Loertscher & Riordan (2019), while Cassidey et al. explicitly analyze in-house capability and supplier pricing. In the current model, however, `p-r` is exogenous, making the stated wedge an identity rather than a strategic result.

### P4 search outcome

No exact prior paper using the same `epsilon` uniform bound was found. But the economic mechanism is already central in 2025–2026 AI-learning theory, and the present epsilon result follows from a maximal-capability sufficient condition plus monotonicity. It is therefore classified as a mathematical sharpening, not an independently new mechanism.

## 6. Agentic-`n` search and nested-model test

The current model was analytically inspected with `n=1`. All headline directions survive. Searches did not identify a result in the current paper that requires multi-step execution as a strategic margin. `n` is therefore classified as an amplifier/scaling parameter.

## 7. Numerical sanity search related to P3 wording

A private numerical check used an admissible recovery technology `rho(H)=1-exp(-gamma H)` and searched parameter sets with both private and technological interior cutoffs. A counterexample was found in which the cutoff gap `s_T*-s_P*` increases between `n=2` and `n=3` but decreases between `n=3` and `n=4`, while the direct price wedge `n(p-r)` continues to increase. This confirms that only the direct price/objective wedge is globally established by the existing proof.

The check is a sanity/counterexample exercise, not a contribution and not a replacement for proof.
