## Use in this project

- brainstorming and structuring arguments;
- drafting and revising manuscript prose;
- organizing the literature discussion and journal positioning;
- checking algebraic derivations and proposition logic;
- sharpening statements of propositions and proofs;
- preparing and editing LaTeX source and submission materials.

The human author remains responsible for deciding what appears in the manuscript and must independently verify equations, proofs, citations, factual claims, and the final submission package.
